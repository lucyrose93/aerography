Dr Lucy Sabin

Researcher, writer, artist, and movement practitioner.

I'm especially fascinated by topics related to the atmosphere, such as weather and climate, and how we perceive and value nature through our bodies and feelings. My academic research is situated in the fields of cultural geography and environmental humanities. But I also use creative methods in my research, like exhibition design or collage.

In my capacity as a Research Fellow in the Digital Humanities Lab at the University of Sussex, I'm currently exploring how people experience and relate to rewilding landscapes through walking interviews and contemplative inquiry with local communities. Embodied practice is also part of my life outside research; I first qualified as a yoga teacher in 2017 and offer classes in Brighton.

Have an idea to share?

I work on a consultancy basis with research teams and institutions on creative engagement, communication, production, and co-design. I’m a member of Artists’ Union England.
