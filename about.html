Dr Lucy Sabin

I'm a researcher, writer, and artist. As a Research Fellow at the University of Sussex, I explore how people experience and relate to nature recovery.

My work combines interviews and focus groups with creative approaches such as filmmaking and collage. I'm interested in weather, atmosphere, and the ways we experience places through our bodies and senses.

Embodied practice is also part of my life outside research. I first qualified as a yoga teacher in 2017 and teach classes in Brighton.

Why aerography? Aerography combines the Greek aēr ('air') with -graphia ('writing', 'drawing' or 'describing'). It reflects my interest in using creative and embodied approaches to explore overlooked aspects of everyday life: the invisible forces, sensory experiences and relationships that shape how environments are felt, understood and inhabited.
