Dr Lucy Sabin

Lucy Sabin (she / her) is an artistic researcher working at the intersection of environmental relations, culture and lived experience. Lucy uses participatory arts, design, film, and other creative methods to explore questions of social and ecological justice. She has written about air pollution, ecofeminism, speculative ecology, Saharan dust, sea breezes, breathing worlds, and lichens among other beings.

Why aerography? This term comes from the Greek aēr (“air”) and -graphia (“writing”, “drawing” or “describing”). A nod to Lucy's Geography PhD, it reflects her longstanding interest in atmospheres—both material and emotional— and in using artistic research to make less tangible experiences and relationships perceptible.
